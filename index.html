About Us

We Started With a Simple Question

In early 2026, a small group of community members in Kampala sat together and asked: “How can we support each other better?” That question became Damera Community Initiative Ltd.

We are new, volunteer-led, and guided by the belief that every person — regardless of nationality, gender, religion or background — deserves a chance to learn, heal, and lead.

Our work focuses on education, mental health, community media, youth empowerment, refugee integration, and peaceful coexistence between refugees and host communities.

Our Journey

Listening First: Our Foundation in Kampala

Damera was not built in an office — it was built in a conversation. On March 1, 2026, we held our first community consultation at Gion Hotel in Kampala.

Community members, youth, women, refugee leaders and local partners came together to share what they were struggling with and what they hoped to see change. We listened. That day became the foundation of our programs.
